💕 To My Dearest Ayushi 💕

Ayushi,

I do not know how to begin without my heart feeling heavier and softer at the same time. Loving you has been the most beautiful and the most vulnerable thing I have ever done. You have become a part of my everyday thoughts, my silent prayers, my smallest smiles. Even on the hardest days, when everything feels uncertain and distant, the thought of you still feels like home.

There are moments when the distance, the situations, the circumstances try to convince me that love should be easier than this. But then I remember your voice, your laughter, the way you look at me, and I realize that nothing meaningful has ever been easy. You are not just someone I love. You are the person my heart chose without fear and without calculation.

Sometimes I wish I could hold your hands while reading this to you. I wish I could look into your eyes and tell you how deeply you matter to me. You are the first person I think about when something good happens and the only one I want to talk to when everything feels overwhelming. My days feel incomplete without you in them.

If love is about choosing someone every single day, then I choose you. Not just on the days when everything feels perfect, but on the days when we are tired, confused, or hurting. I choose you in the quiet moments and in the storms. I choose you when it is easy and when it is not.

This Valentine's Day is not about flowers or grand gestures for me. It is about the promise in my heart that no matter how complicated life becomes, my feelings for you remain simple and true. I love you in a way that scares me because it is real. I love you in a way that makes me stronger and more fragile at the same time.

If I could ask for anything today, it would be more time with you, more memories, more chances to grow together. I do not want a perfect story. I just want ours. And I want it for as long as life allows.

Happy Valentine's Day, Ayushi. You are my love, my peace, and the part of my heart that I never want to lose.

Forever Yours 💕
